FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing depends on volume, weight, item type, and access. For many jobs, we can provide a fast quote after a quick email or photo review.

Do I need to move everything outside first?

No. We can remove items from inside the property, garage, attic, basement, office, or yard. Curbside pickup is also an option when appropriate.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Yes, when availability allows. Contact us and we will let you know the fastest available appointment window.

What items can you not take?

We may not accept hazardous or restricted materials such as chemicals, fuel, asbestos, biohazards, and certain specialty waste items. Contact us if you are unsure.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Yes. Photos help a lot for large cleanouts, bulky-item pickups, and jobs where access or volume is unclear.
